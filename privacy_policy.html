# Privacy Policy for LatheWorks **Effective Date: June 24, 2026** This Privacy Policy describes how LatheWorks ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and to collecting as little information as possible. ## 1. Data Collection and Usage **LatheWorks does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data of its own.** We do not require you to create an account with us, provide an email address, or enter any personally identifiable information to use the application. We operate no servers and receive none of your data. ## 2. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing LatheWorks offers optional paid subscription tiers (Lite, Full, and Pro). These subscriptions are purchased and managed entirely through **Google Play Billing**, a service provided by Google. When you buy, restore, or manage a subscription: - The purchase is processed by **Google Play**, not by LatheWorks. We never see or handle your payment card, bank details, or Google account credentials. - To complete a purchase and to verify or restore an existing subscription, the app communicates with Google Play over the internet. During this process Google receives and processes purchase and subscription information (for example, the product purchased and your subscription status) in accordance with **Google's own Privacy Policy** (https://policies.google.com/privacy). - The only information LatheWorks stores as a result is a single local flag on your device recording which tier you own so the app can unlock the corresponding features. This flag stays on your device. If you never purchase a subscription, the app makes no billing-related network requests. ## 3. Network Use Aside from the Google Play Billing communication described in Section 2, LatheWorks does not require an internet connection to operate and does not transmit your programs, part data, tool libraries, or any other content you create. All engineering and G-code work is performed locally on your device. ## 4. Analytics and Tracking LatheWorks does not use any analytics software, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, or third-party tracking mechanisms. We do not monitor how you use the app. ## 5. File and Storage Access LatheWorks can export the G-code programs you generate so you can transfer them to a CNC machine controller — for example, by writing a `.nc` or `.cnc` file to a USB/OTG drive — and can import contour files (such as DXF or TXT) that you choose. The app requests **no broad storage permission**. File import and export use Android's built-in **Storage Access Framework** document picker: you explicitly choose each file to import and each destination to export to, in the system picker. The app only reads the single file you select (import is strictly read-only — nothing on the source is modified) and only writes to the destination you choose (export). It does not browse, scan, upload, index, or transmit your files, and it cannot see any file you have not explicitly selected. Your programs, tool libraries, and settings created inside the app are saved to the app's own private storage on your device. ## 6. Local Storage All data generated within the app, including your G-code programs and tool libraries, is saved directly to your device. We have no access to your programs, parts data, or any other information entered into the application. Your work belongs entirely to you and remains on your device (except for the subscription information handled by Google Play as described in Section 2). ## 7. GDPR Compliance LatheWorks itself does not collect, process, or store any personal data, and so aligns with the GDPR principles of data minimization and user privacy. Subscription payments are handled by Google Play; Google's processing of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. ## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app's updated description or within this document in future releases. ## 9.
